About This Work

I am an energy and sustainability professional with experience in generating quantitative evidence to support energy investment, energy transition, climate policy, and sustainable development across Africa. My work combines technical energy analysis, technoeconomic assessment, regulatory engagement, and stakeholder collaboration to translate complex engineering and financial data into practical policy and investment recommendations.

My experience spans industrial energy systems, equipment assessment, electricity consumption analysis, energy efficiency, renewable energy integration, digital monitoring, and institutional capacity building. My work has spanned collaboration with government regulators, industry, and development stakeholders to support evidence-based decision making aligned with global sustainability and climate objectives.

In summary, my work generates data-driven evidence on how electricity end use can influence use of fossil fuel, investment decisions, and decarbonization pathways towards a just energy transition. I have analyzed more than 1,000 electricity, equipment performance, and fossil fuel consumption data sets; this has proved that high-quality energy data can guide countries, organizatons and C&I facilities in funding energy projects with certainity.

Lighting Systems Demand Assessment

How can organizations use lighting system efficiency to reduce electricity demand while contributing to industrial decarbonization?

Purpose

Lighting systems represent a significant proportion of facility energy demand. This analysis evaluated energy performance, operating schedules, and lighting fixture efficiency.

Data Assessment

  • Connected loads
  • Operating hours
  • Electricity consumption profile
  • Retrofit potential

Findings Outcome

The analysis demonstrated how end-use efficiency can reduce electricity use while indirectly reducing carbon emissions and operational costs. At an organizational level, these findings informed investment prioritization, electricity demand reduction strategies, and decarbonization impact. On a broader aspect, the evidence contributes to industrial energy efficiency programs supporting Kenya's energy transition objectives.
